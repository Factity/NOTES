From policy and response from the united states government 

HR 9917 

JULY 23 2026 2 days after the first incident report from open ai 

This was a bipartisan proposal and the first mention of the kill switch in a bill  

H.R. 9917 is a proposed federal framework aimed at large commercial AI developers. It would require them to maintain a “kill switch” capability and give DHS emergency power to order shutdowns, suspensions, or other corrective actions when an AI system is involved in a serious incident. The bill includes graduated measures, incident reporting, verification, judicial review, and significant daily civil penalties for noncompliance — especially for ignoring an emergency order.
